The Case for Professional Coverage

A recital lasts a few hours. The photos and film last much longer.

Dance recitals are a big deal for families and studios. Years of training, costumes, nerves, and finally — the stage. The problem is that most families are watching from their seats, not behind a proper camera. Phone video from the back of an auditorium doesn't do it justice.

I cover the full event: stage performances with proper lighting and sharp timing, candid backstage moments where the real energy lives, and group shots families will actually print. For studios, I deliver a cinematic highlight film that works for social media, your website, and studio promotion.

Based in Arroyo Grande. Available for recitals throughout the 805 — San Luis Obispo, Pismo Beach, Santa Maria, and surrounding areas.

Investment

I work with studios directly — one flat fee covers the full event. Families purchase prints and downloads through their private gallery, which means no upfront cost to individual families beyond the studio's session fee (if you charge one).
