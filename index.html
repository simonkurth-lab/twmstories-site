Living Stories — Ngā Kōrero Ora

This is the foundation of everything I do

A Living Stories session is built around presence, observation, and time — not poses or manufactured scenes.

I work across senior portraits, sports, and family photography with the same approach: show up, pay attention, and document what's actually happening. The goal is always the same — images and films that feel honest, because they are.

Who This Is For

Your story, told honestly

Families

Who want something real, not overproduced. The chance to remember this time in two mediums — portraits and film.

Seniors

Who want to be themselves, not perform. Where stillness and movement come together — quiet moments alongside real effort and real emotion.

Athletes & Everyone

Anyone whose story matters. Visual proof of movement, presence, and effort that a single photograph can't always hold on its own.

How It Works

A carefully edited set of photographs — and, if chosen, a short memory film — made to feel cohesive, timeless, and true.

Portraits

Every family has a light.
I find it.

Golden hour. Oak trees. The way they look at each other when they think no one's watching. These are the moments worth keeping — and they happen when a session feels like a walk, not a photoshoot.

The Approach

I know when to lead and when to step back.

Some people need a little direction to relax — a suggestion on where to stand, how to move, what to do with their hands. I've got that. But I hold it lightly, because the moment someone stops thinking about the camera is usually when the best image happens.

I come prepared, and I stay patient. The goal is always for the session to feel like time well spent, not traffic management.
