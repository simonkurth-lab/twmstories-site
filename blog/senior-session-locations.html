Where We'll Shoot Your Story

Here are the spots I shoot most for senior sessions. Click a location to see it on the map, then tap "Get Directions" whenever you're ready to scout it yourself. If none of these work, tell me what you have in mind and we'll figure it out.

Wilmar Street, North Pismo
What to Wear
Bring 2–3 changes if you can:
  • Wear what makes you feel comfortable — this comes first
  • Something that's just you — favorite jacket, jeans, casual
  • One look that's a little more dressed up
  • Solid colors over busy patterns or big logos — they photograph cleaner and keep the focus on you
What to Bring
  • Baseball glove, football, guitar — whatever makes you you, bring it
  • Sport gear, an instrument, anything tied to what you do — it becomes part of the shot
  • A friend or family member is welcome to come along
  • You're welcome to get a shot with your parents too, if you want one for the memories
Session Timeline
Sessions run 60–90 minutes depending on your package. We'll usually work through 1–2 locations, sometimes more if we're chasing a few different looks. Try to arrive a few minutes early so we can walk the spot and talk through what you're picturing before we start.
Questions, or want to talk through your ideas before session day?
Text or call me — happy to help you plan it out.
