Perspective May 2, 2026

Shakes and All

In a world full of AI-generated everything, I choose to leave the imperfections in.

A slight focus shift. A head drifting into frame from someone who didn't know the camera was rolling. A moment that didn't go to plan.

All good. That's real life.

I choose real life and real moments—not because I can't fix them, but because I won't. The human essence gets lost when everything is smoothed out and perfected into something that never actually existed.

The shake. The glance. The unscripted second.

That's the story worth keeping.
