Senior Night · Arroyo Grande Baseball · May 2, 2026

Senior NightFight Back

Arroyo Grande 14
Final
Mission Prep 10
Game Coverage May 2, 2026

Special mention to Sarah Winterberg for capturing so many incredible moments between players and parents, and to Lauri and Heather for all the behind-the-scenes work that makes nights like this actually happen.

This day came faster than I expected. But I'm so thankful I got to be there, to witness my son and his teammates have their moment, and to do it the way this group always has.

Humble. Genuine. No drama, just heart.

Down big, the boys fought back with an 11-run inning, something I've never seen before. That kind of fight says everything about who they are.

Also, much respect to the Mission parents who were celebrating their seniors this week as well. Wishing all of those players nothing but the best moving forward.

Wishing the Eagles all the best in their remaining games.
