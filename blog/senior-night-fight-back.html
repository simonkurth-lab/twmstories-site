Friday night at Arroyo Grande, the scoreboard said one thing. The Eagles said another.

The Comeback

Down 10-3 heading into the fifth — most teams start doing the math. This group never did.

What happened in the bottom of the sixth is the kind of inning you don't forget. Eleven runs. Six hits. And a complete refusal to let the night end that way.

"A complete refusal to let the night end that way."

Brock Paz was everywhere — four hits in five at-bats, a solo home run to center in the first, a triple and two singles when it mattered most. Anders Winter, Jones Stumph, Tane Kurth, Reid Bronson — all collecting hits, all keeping the line moving, every single at-bat.
