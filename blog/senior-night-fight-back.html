Game Coverage May 2, 2026

Friday night at Arroyo Grande, the scoreboard said one thing. The Eagles said another.

What happened in the bottom of the sixth is the kind of inning you don't forget. Eleven runs. Six hits. And a complete refusal to let the night end that way.

The Comeback

"A complete refusal to let the night end that way."

Brock Paz was everywhere — four hits in five at-bats, a solo home run to center in the first, a triple and two singles when it mattered most. Reid Bronson launched one over the fence. So did Jones Stumph. Anders Winter, Tane Kurth — all of them kept the line moving, every single at-bat.

That's fifteen hits as a team. You don't get fifteen hits without everyone contributing. You don't erase an eleven-run deficit without the whole lineup showing up in the same moment.

Final: Arroyo Grande 14, Mission College Prep 10. That's the stuff I love to document.

The game was epic for the amount of offense by both teams.

Honestly — it was a tough day for me personally. Trying to hold it together behind the lens when it's your own kid out there is something else. The day came faster than I expected. But I was so grateful to be there. To witness my son and his teammates have their moment — and to do it the way this group always does. Humble. Genuine. No drama, just heart.

That's who these kids are.

Special mention to Sarah Winterberg for capturing so many incredible shots of players and parents throughout the night — and to Lauri and Heather for all the behind-the-scenes work that makes nights like this actually happen. That stuff doesn't get enough credit.

Wishing the Eagles all the best with their remaining games.

Go Eagles. 🦅
