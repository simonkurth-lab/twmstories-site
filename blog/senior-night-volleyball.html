Senior Night — Volleyball

Some nights you show up with a camera and leave with something more than photos.

This was one of those nights.

I had the privilege of photographing volleyball senior night for several families I've known for a long time. Watching kids I've seen grow up get their moment — walk across that court, flowers in hand, families beside them — doesn't get old. It's an honor every time.

I'm grateful these families let me be there to capture it.

Tomorrow night my own son has his senior night. Baseball.

So I'm sitting with tonight's images feeling reflective and lucky in equal measure — lucky to do this work, lucky to be part of this community, lucky to know these families.
