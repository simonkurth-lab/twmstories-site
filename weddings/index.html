The Approach

Honest coverage. Nothing staged that doesn't need to be.

Most photographers talk about being unobtrusive. I mean it. Portraits and family formals happen efficiently — they have to. The rest of the day, I'm in the margins: the getting-ready room, the quiet moment before the ceremony, the table where your grandparents are laughing. That's where the photographs that matter usually come from.

For couples who want a film as well — I build them around the actual day, not a template. The vows, the toasts, the moments in between. Set to music, edited to feel timeless rather than cinematic for its own sake.

I'm based in Arroyo Grande and serve the full Central Coast — San Luis Obispo, Pismo Beach, the wine country around Paso Robles, and venues from the beach to the hills. I'm also open to destination work with proper lead time.

The Process

Simple from the first message to the final delivery.

02
