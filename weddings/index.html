Weddings

My hope is to create images and films that allow you to experience those moments again, not just remember them.

My role isn't to direct every moment. It's to notice them.

The quiet moments before the ceremony. The laughter between family and friends. The emotion that often goes unnoticed. The glance across the room. The hug that lasts a little longer than expected.

Some moments deserve to be remembered. Others deserve to be felt again, almost immediately.

My approach brings together documentary photography, cinematic filmmaking, and editorial storytelling, preserving your wedding with the care it deserves while giving you something meaningful to share before the memories have even begun to settle.

Every wedding is led and edited personally by me, because I believe every story deserves the same care from beginning to end.

If your plans look a little different, I'd love to hear about them. Every wedding is unique, and I'm always happy to create a collection that fits your day.

How additional coverage works

Every wedding has its own rhythm. If your timeline calls for more time together, additional coverage can be arranged in advance, subject to availability. For Premium Hybrid, additional coverage is subject to second-photographer availability.

Additional hours extend your photography or filming coverage without changing the included film lengths or other collection deliverables.

Nine- and ten-hour coverage can be arranged using your selected eight-hour collection as the starting point. Longer coverage can be discussed individually.

Wedding films are thoughtfully crafted, music-driven highlights of your day. Complete ceremony recordings, full speeches, raw footage, and unedited recordings are not included. Selected natural sounds or spoken moments may be incorporated where they enhance the story.

Wedding FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

How many photographs will we receive?

Every wedding is different. Photography-only collections generally deliver approximately 50 thoughtfully edited images per hour of coverage. Hybrid collections vary because I'm personally capturing both photography and film, and Premium Hybrid includes a second photographer for broader coverage. These are estimates, not guaranteed minimums. My priority is a complete, carefully curated story rather than repetitive photographs.

What happens in the first 48 hours?

You'll receive The First 48, a custom editorial preview of your wedding. Photography and hybrid collections include a 20-slide social-media-ready story, and hybrid collections also incorporate short cinematic video moments. Film Only includes a short video-led preview.

When will our complete photographs arrive?

Typically within 6–8 weeks.

When will our finished film arrive?

Typically within 8–12 weeks.

How do we reserve our wedding date?

A signed agreement and booking retainer secure the date. The retainer is applied toward the collection total, and payment terms are outlined in the agreement.

Do you travel?

Yes. TWM Stories serves San Luis Obispo County, Paso Robles, and the wider Central Coast. Additional travel arrangements are discussed before booking.

Will you direct us during portraits?

Yes, when helpful. My approach balances gentle guidance with natural documentary coverage so your wedding feels like a celebration rather than an all-day photoshoot.

Can we add additional coverage?

Additional coverage is available at $350 per hour, subject to availability and agreement.
