The Process

Simple, intentional, and personal

01

We talk first

A conversation about who your senior is — what drives them, what they care about, what this year has meant. That shapes everything that follows, from location to timing to how the film is built.

02

I observe and capture

Light direction when it helps. Then I step back and let real moments happen. Sessions are calm, never rushed. The best images almost always come from the second hour.

03

You receive your story

A curated gallery of edited portraits delivered privately. And a memory film if you chose one — built around your senior specifically, not a template.
