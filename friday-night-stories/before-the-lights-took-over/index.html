Before the Lights Took Over

There are only so many Friday nights.

The season began on the road for Arroyo Grande. A week later, they were home — and there are few things quite like the first Friday night back on your own field.

On this particular Friday, even the sky seemed ready for it.

Before the stadium lights completely took over, orange, red and gold stretched across the horizon as the stands filled and the teams prepared below. For a few minutes, the spectacle in the sky seemed every bit as impressive as the one unfolding on the field.

From the sideline, it was hard not to stop and take it in. A few looks between photographers pretty much said everything.

Wow.

Then the lights came on, the stands filled out, and attention turned to football.

There was an intensity on the field from the beginning, helped along by a home crowd more than ready to welcome its team back. There is something different about football at home — familiar surroundings, familiar faces in the stands, and the energy that builds as another Friday night gets underway.

For the players, these nights are finite.

Having dropped my own high school graduate off at college this week, that feels a little more real to me right now. For the seniors especially, there are only so many opportunities left to put on the uniform, run onto their home field and spend a Friday night alongside their teammates.

Maybe that's also why I feel so fortunate to be on the sideline with a camera.

It's genuinely an honor to help capture these memories for our community, our friends and, most importantly, the players themselves. There aren't many things that make me happier than seeing a player excited because a big moment — their moment — was caught.

Because when you're in the middle of a season, it's easy for the next game to simply feel like the next game.

Another warmup.
Another kickoff.
Another night under the lights.

Until, eventually, there isn't another one.

There will be scores, big plays, wins and losses as the season unfolds. But every home game is also a collection of moments that somebody will want to remember.

And on this particular Friday, before the lights took over, the sky made sure this one would be hard to forget.

— Simon Kurth | TWM Stories
