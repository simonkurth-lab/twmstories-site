Ring Day

Simon Kurth · TWM Stories · Arroyo Grande Eagles Football · CIF Division 2 Champions
← Friday Night Stories

Championship rings only get handed out once. This was that day.

The Arroyo Grande Eagles got together this week to celebrate their CIF Division 2 Championship season and receive their championship rings.

It wasn't a huge production. Just teammates, coaches, families, and one more chance for this group to be together after a memorable season.

"Just teammates, coaches, families, and one more chance for this group to be together after a memorable season."

What stood out most was how relaxed everything felt. Players catching up, laughing, looking at each other's rings, and talking about moments from the year. After months of practices, games, and pressure, this was simply about enjoying what they accomplished together.

· · ·

Championship teams don't come around often, and you could tell this group appreciated that.

Congratulations to the CIF Division 2 Champion Arroyo Grande Eagles on an incredible season and a well-earned ring day celebration.

— Simon Kurth, TWM Stories
